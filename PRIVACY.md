# Privacy policy

Pixel 8 Battery Health. Last updated October 26, 2026.

## Battery data

Battery readings stay on your phone. The app has no account, does not send your battery data anywhere and does not store anything about you.

## Ads

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects device data such as your advertising ID, IP address, a general location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy choices" at the bottom of the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings under Security & privacy > More privacy settings > Ads.
