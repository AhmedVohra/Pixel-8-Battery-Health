Privacy policy

Pixel 8 Battery Health for Android. Last updated November 10, 2026.

Battery data

Battery readings stay on your phone, and so does the battery use per app that the "Since last charge" card shows. The app has no account, does not send your battery data anywhere and does not store anything about you.

Ads

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects device data such as your advertising ID, IP address, a general location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy choices" at the bottom of the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings under Security & privacy > More privacy settings > Ads.

Permissions

The app reads battery information from Android. The optional BATTERY_STATS, DUMP and PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS permissions, which you grant yourself with adb, are used only to read battery capacity, health score, battery dates and Android's estimate of how much battery each app and part of the phone used since the last charge. To name the apps in that list, the app looks up installed apps that have a home screen icon. The INTERNET permission is used only by the ad SDK.
